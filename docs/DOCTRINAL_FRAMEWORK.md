## Evidence firewall
1. First establish the source Hebrew/Greek word, morphology, syntax, historical semantic range and context.
2. Then choose a source-supported near-interlinear English expression under all existing owner lexical and tense locks.
3. **Only after that**, discuss the binitarian interpretation under an explicitly labeled theological/canonical note, distinguishing disputed positions.
4. Do not mechanically interpret every Hebrew `רוּחַ` or Greek `πνεῦμα` as Jesus, even though the owner affirms that the Lord Jesus is the Spirit. Actual verse interpretation still needs textual evidence.
5. No source gloss or purported homonym may be invented solely to support a Christological or other doctrinal conclusion.

The owner has also made this a permanent convention: select *in*, *with*, *by*, *through*, or another translation of Hebrew `בְּ` **by the local grammatical context**, not by a blanket equation. A relevant, source-defensible alternative can be shown **directly under** its English anchor. Do not always put *with* beneath *in*; do not put alternatives inline as `in/with`. Uncertain cases are `REVIEW_REQUIRED`.
