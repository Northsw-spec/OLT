# OLT-017B — Second scholarly research wave: four difficult Genesis 1:2 words

**RESEARCH, NOT A FINAL ENGLISH LEXICON DECISION.** The Hebrew below is sourced from the pinned OSHB/WLC Genesis 1:2 original-word dossier, and every studied item is tied to the actual original source-token ID. Full paper review is NOT claimed where only the abstract is accessible.

## Tohu (source lemma 8414, token 01aPd)

The draft word **waste** is compatible with an important contextual range of BDB, but other senses include **formlessness, desolation and emptiness**. For the expression *tohu wabohu*, Jeremiah 4:23 repeats the sequence in a context of devastation; the two Hebrew nouns must not be merged into a single lemma. A 2010 study by Robin Routledge acknowledges theological tension between creation of all things and the imagery of imposing order on chaos. Moș (2017) reviews differences among Jewish, patristic and contemporary readings. Neither discussion establishes that *tohu* literally means 'pre-existing matter'.

Sources: [BDB 8414](https://biblehub.com/bdb/8414.htm); [Routledge, *Tyndale Bulletin* (abstract)](https://doaj.org/article/6fa0339d241c4cd8a5318b88b2ae22da); [Moș (abstract)](https://studia.reviste.ubbcluj.ro/index.php/subbtheologiaorthodoxa/article/view/3891).

## Bohu (source lemma 922, token 01eYX)

The draft **and emptiness** preserves the original conjunctive waw and a separate source noun. BDB records *bohu* as **emptiness/voidness**, used in Gen 1:2, Jer 4:23 and Isa 34:11 alongside *tohu*. Its striking phonetic pairing with *tohu* is a candidate for stylistic commentary; it is **not an etymologically proven homonym identity**. The reported three examples are not evidence that these two roots mean the same thing.

Sources: [BDB 922](https://www.studylight.org/lexicons/eng/hebrew/922.html); [Moș (abstract)](https://studia.reviste.ubbcluj.ro/index.php/subbtheologiaorthodoxa/article/view/3891).

## Tehom (source lemma 8415, token 01PB6)

The draft **the Deep** is a contextual translation of BDB's sea / abyss / deep. Scholars debate the history of Near Eastern creation symbolism, including possible connections and literary echoes with *Tiamat*. The fact that the words can be compared does **not** establish that Genesis 1:2 directly writes a proper deity name. A research dissertation surveys how simplistic identification can misrepresent the wider Hebrew use of *tehom*. Therefore any link to Tiamat belongs in a **carefully qualified background note**, not a purple/blue word pair claiming identical lexemes.

Sources: [BDB 8415](https://biblehub.com/bdb/8415.htm); [UC eScholarship academic repository](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/0rn492nd).

## Merachephet (source lemma 7363 b, token 01yNB)

In Genesis 1:2 the form **מְרַחֶפֶת** is annotated as **Piel active participle, feminine singular** (`HVprfsa`). **Hovering** follows the attested Piel range. BDB also cites Deuteronomy 32:11, where the hover/flutter verb occurs in bird imagery, but the form there is a different conjugation. The Piel and Qal sense groupings must not be conflated. *Hovering* in Genesis is NOT a *wayyiqtol*, and no generic rule requires English simple past for this participle.

Sources: [BDB 7363](https://biblehub.com/bdb/7363.htm); [Genesis 1:2 morphological display](https://seethebible.org/bible/genesis/1/2).
