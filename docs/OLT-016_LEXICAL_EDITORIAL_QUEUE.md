# OLT-016: Editorial word-family and homonym review queue

This source-first checkpoint builds one lexical research record for every one of the 97 exact observed OSHB lemma IDs in Genesis 1. Every record contains actual original word-token IDs, verse addresses, source morphology, original Hebrew word, English research fragments, verified BDB/Strong source IDs, owner-locked family where established, editorial tasks and a homonym-link slot positioned BELOW the English word.

The 94 English families without an owner lock still need their meaning and morphological usage reviewed. Variations in English fragments can reflect prefix, article, pronominal suffix, conjugation or context; they do NOT automatically prove lexical polysemy or homonymy.

No cross-reference or Christian theological association is promoted to literal Hebrew lexical meaning without independent evidence. This pipeline has no verse-approval power and no capacity to certify the complete 9,519-source-ID Old Testament English lexicon.

The source import, automated coverage QA and English candidate review are distinct milestones. This checkpoint creates research tasks, not final Bible text. All approved homonym links remain empty until separately reviewed.
