# OLT-010 — Genesis 1:6–10 source-first translation research

**Status:** Five preliminary English verse research proposals; 63 of 63 actual source word tokens and 11 OSHB-tagged wayyiqtol forms are accounted for. These are NOT independently certified or published Bible verses.

## Preliminary proposed English (research, not owner approval)
6. And God — He said: Let a firmament be in the midst of the waters; and let [him] be separating between waters to waters.

7. And God — He made the firmament, and He separated between the waters which from beneath to the firmament, and between the waters which from above to the firmament; and so was.

8. And God — He called to the firmament Heavens; and was evening, and was morning: day second.

9. And God — He said: Let the waters from beneath the heavens be gathered unto place one, and let the dry ground be seen; and so was.

10. And God — He called to the dry ground Earth, and to the gathering of the waters He called Seas; and God — He saw that — good.

## Verified evidence boundaries
- OSHB/WLC original source word counts: 11, 17, 10, 13, 12. All word IDs, exact original lemma annotations and morphology tags are checked. Each source token has original Hebrew text and dictionary refs.
- **Owner locks:** רָקִיעַ => **firmament**, עשה => **made**, never shaped. Wayyiqtol finite verb forms render in English simple past.
- Genesis 1:6 `yehi` (HVqj3ms) is jussive; `vihi` (HC/Vqi3ms) is OSHB-tagged imperfect with a proposed volitive English sense in context; `mavdil` (HVhrmsa) is Hiphil participle, not narrative past. `[him]` is visibly supplied English for the grammatically masculine firmament, not a separately written Hebrew word.
- Genesis 1:7 `vayyaas` (HC/Vqw3ms) is wayyiqtol and English **made**, not shaped. Et remains an explicit zero-surface direct object marker.
- Genesis 1:8 `yom sheni` includes an ordinal second, different from Genesis 1:5 cardinal `echad`; OLT's awkward `day second` is intentional lexical fidelity.
- Genesis 1:9 `yiqqawu` (HVNj3mp) is Niphal jussive (third masculine plural), `veteraeh` (HC/VNi3fs) is Niphal imperfect with proposed contextual volitive translation. Neither is wayyiqtol. `yabbashah` is dry ground; `maqom` place.
- Genesis 1:10 `miqveh` is the gathering/collection of the waters. It is not automatically a proved linguistic identity with later ritual mikveh terminology simply because of source consonants. The second `qara` (HVqp3ms) is qatal; first `vayyiqra` is wayyiqtol. The clause `ki tov` is literally that good and its grammatical completion cannot be imported from Genesis 1:4 without research.
- No English neutral **it** appears in research verse lines.
- No new under-word homonym links or Christological proofs are invented. This is a **lexical research** checkpoint, not final editorial approval.
