# OLT-018 — Genesis 1:1 owner's bracketed theological supplement

**Authoritative MAIN English research line (owner-selected wording):**

> In the Beginning [of His Power] God — He shaped the Heavens and the Earth.

The bracketed phrase is **not** an independent Hebrew translation of a word in Gen 1:1 and must be clearly footnoted. The first verse contains seven OSHB/WLC original word tokens; no "power" lexeme or separate "His" possessive occurs among them.

**Verse footnote (English, required, source-aware):**

> [of His Power] is an editorial theological supplement, not a translation of any Hebrew words in Genesis 1:1. Hebrew בְּרֵאשִׁית (bə-rēʾšît) means "in the beginning" (its syntax is debated). Genesis 49:3 reads וְרֵאשִׁית אֹונִי, "and the beginning of my strength"; Deuteronomy 21:17 reads רֵאשִׁית אֹנוֹ, "the beginning of his strength." In both parallels, strength/vigor is the separate Hebrew noun אֹון, absent from Genesis 1:1; the possessive "His" is also supplied. The association is theological and intertextual, not a lexical meaning of rēʾšît.

**Supporting original text witnesses:** [Genesis 49:3, Hebrew](https://www.chabad.org/library/bible_cdo/aid/8244); [Deuteronomy 21:17, Hebrew WLC](https://openbible.com/text/deuteronomy/21-17.htm).

**Syntactic research caution:** The opening בְּרֵאשִׁית is grammatically debated. See [Kantor, 2025, Cambridge accepted abstract](https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/items/bce448c5-e43e-416d-89da-5876ead16bc3). The bracketed addition is an owner's interpretive decision, not a scholarly claim that the Gen 1:1 text includes "strength".
