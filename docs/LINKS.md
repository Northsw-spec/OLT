Categories: HOMONYM, HOMOGRAPH, POLYSEMY, SHARED_ROOT, NAME_ASSOCIATION, PARONOMASIA, DIRECT_INTERTEXT, TYPOLOGY, DISPUTED.

For a linked English main word colored **#5B277D**, display the blue **#2463A6** secondary word **on a distinct line DIRECTLY BELOW**, bound to the main word. No inline “Noah/Rest” or “Pitch/Atonement” in final manuscript. Each must have an English scholarly note stating precise original-script spelling, type, cited verses, confidence and limitations.

Suggested examples are not all homonyms:
- Noah / Rest: name association; Gen 5:29 comfort is wordplay from another root.
- Rest for dove's foot (Gen 8:9) / no rest for sole of foot (Deut 28:65): direct intertext.
- Serpent / Divination: shared Hebrew consonants נחש but distinct noun and verb.
- Pitch / Atonement: כפר relation disputed; do not misstate the verb for coating Noah's ark.
- Ark / Christ: typology involving 1 Pet 3:20–21; never assert the boat was physically cross-shaped.

Reject unverified lexical identity and absent verse anchors. Color by itself is not proof of semantic equivalence.
