# OLT-013 — Genesis 1:21–25 Hebrew source-first research

All 74 original Hebrew source tokens in Gen 1:21–25 are accounted for. 9 original OSHB source-tagged wayyiqtol forms; other original imperative, jussive, qatal and participle forms stay separate.

Key items: `וַיִּבְרָא` → **shaped** (bara owner lock) versus `וַיַּעַשׂ` → **made** (asah owner lock). `תנינים` may be understood as great sea creatures, dragons/sea monsters in traditional lexical ranges; 'sea-creatures' remains a contextual candidate, not a new approved lexical family. `nefesh chayyah` is literally living soul/creature, retaining two source words. Gen 1:22 imperative commands must not be treated as narrative past; `yirev` is jussive. `otam` has an actual 3mp object-pronoun suffix, not a blank accusative marker. Feminine suffixes `lemīnah` remain `her kind` under the OLT English grammatical-gender policy.

A **research-only Christian canonical cross-reference** from the divine agent in Gen 1:21 to John 1:3 is not a Hebrew-Greek lexical homonym and is never automatically placed under an English word. All future verified homonym display remains vertical *under* the English word, never inline `/`.
