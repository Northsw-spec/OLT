# OLT-012 — Genesis 1:16–20 source-first research

**Scope:** 59 actual Masoretic word tokens, 6 source-tagged wayyiqtol, research-not-publication status. Original words/lemma/morphology and source-linked Strong/BDB identifiers are derived from pinned OSHB and stored in each alignment.

Distinctive safeguards:
- Asah on Gen 1:16 renders **made**, never shaped.
- Et with no suffix in Gen 1:16 has no English lexical surface, but `אֹתָם` (`HTo/Sp3mp`) in Gen 1:17 requires English object pronoun **them**. A blanket zero-gloss rule for every `853` form would be wrong.
- Two great light-bearing bodies (ma'orot, BDB/Strong evidence) are not conflated with the lexeme `or` of first-day light. Their rule/dominion is attested separately.
- Gen 1:18 infinitives `limshol` and `lehavdil` remain infinitive constructions, not narrative *wayyiqtol*.
- Gen 1:20 yishretsu is source-tagged Qal imperfect and yeofef is Polel imperfect. Both are proposed as volitives from contextual speech, not as past-tense verbs.
- Wordplay `yishretsu / sherets` and `of / yeofef` is a **research candidate**, not automatic identification as an independently verified homonym pair.
- The OLT main text retains its no-neuter-`it` rule and places any future evidenced homonym alternatives **under** the English word, never separated by an inline slash.
- Potential Christological parallels may be evaluated at canonical-exegesis stage. Nothing here invents a root link solely because of a theological analogy.
