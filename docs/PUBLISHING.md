# Print and manuscript standard

English main text ~16 pt; pointed Hebrew / NT Greek ~13.5 pt; English scholarly footnotes ~11 pt; A4, legible serif and generous line spacing. Refine after visual inspection. The reference ODT is a layout example **only**. True footnotes must appear at the bottom of the relevant page, with genuine references and script citations. Place the subordinate blue gloss below its purple main word, with a note marker. Preserve RTL Hebrew vowels and Greek accents. Check monochrome readability, overflow, line breaks, page margins and printed appearance.

Use a single note on Bible page one: “Hebrew nouns are grammatically masculine or feminine. This translation may preserve these forms through English gendered pronouns or bracketed additions. Grammatical gender does not necessarily denote bodily sex or personhood.” Never repeat the general note in each chapter.

No older ODT, PDF or draft is a source of verified lexical facts. No chapter may be published without full actual-source alignment, review sign-off and visually inspected ODT/PDF.

## OLT-033 — Bible-wide names-underwords typesetting and approval gate

At every occurrence with verified meaningful source support, display the name in the purple primary tier and the English name meaning/association in a smaller blue tier **vertically below**. Multiword explanations are permitted but must wrap/fit legibly without changing the reading order, inventing printed source terms or moving a footnote away from its name anchor. Every such underword has a status-qualified scholarly note; repeated occurrences can share a note definition while keeping individual source-token anchors.

Names of people, cities, settlements, places, divine titles and unusual biblical designations are all in scope in **both Testaments**. The rendering pipeline MUST distinguish direct name meaning, naming wordplay, traditional/hypothetical place etymology, and owner canonical theological interpretation; the last may appear as **clearly marked interpretation only**, not as disguised Hebrew/Greek etymology. A disputed or opaque name cannot be published with an invented meaning merely to fulfill a visual quota.

The underword system must coexist with the single OLT-022 Gen 1:1 In/with prefix link and preserved bracketed [of His Power] note. Before release: independently verify original-script occurrence IDs, the NT RP2018 source, the verse-by-verse coverage inventory, possible name senses and notes, actual A4 wrapping, accessible reading order, color/monochrome legibility, and true bottom-of-page footnotes. Until these gates pass, any HTML/ODT/PDF name-underword sample is **RESEARCH ONLY, PUBLICATION BLOCKED**.
- **Noah/Rest** should be shown as an independent vertically stacked name meaning where the original occurrence confirms Noah; 'comfort' belongs in its explanatory note, not as a falsely identical Hebrew root.

## OLT-034 — multilingual publication gates and typesetting

A green core CI run does not authorize a Bible translation in ANY target language. Each language repository separately validates exact original source edition and immutable revision, completeness of source-token mapping, lemma/sense decisions, grammatical adequacy, gender/tense conventions appropriate to that language, spelling and typography (including Ethiopic for Amharic, Japanese scripts and segmentation, Simplified/Traditional Chinese and Icelandic diacritics), translated names/underwords and footnotes, printed A4/PDF/ODT/HTML reading order, and independent review approval. The initial six locale profiles contain ZERO translated Scripture words.

The common OLT core gives traceable, original-language source evidence and English research reference only. Each locale renders from its own reviewed choices. Preserve existing OLT Gen 1:1 original-language fidelity, owner-defined Gen 1:1 In/with underword and [of His Power] note, and exact approved English wording without projecting English grammar onto Japanese, Chinese, Amharic or Icelandic. Independent per-locale releases remain BLOCKED.

## OLT-035 — publication blocked on original-source attestations

Every target-language release must be traceable to a pinned original Hebrew/Aramaic/Greek textual edition, exact source-token alignment, lexeme/morphology and evidence. Git byte-identity checks prevent accidental corpus drift but do NOT replace independent linguistic review, proper Greek word segmentation, cross-edition collation, authorized editorial decisions or print QA.

The first RP2018 Greek 3 John import is **14 authentic Greek verse strings without word-level IDs or morphology**. It cannot legally or technically be promoted to a finished source-verified Greek lexicon, full NT or publishable Greek-target translation. Preserve all owner decisions and project-wide release blocker.
