# OLT-021 — Authoritative owner correction: the eternal, uncreated Son

**Date:** 30 October 2026  
**Status:** Binding OLT owner-approved theological and editorial interpretation  
**Supersedes:** Any assistant statement that reads the owner's phrase *“the First of God's Creation”* as asserting that the Son was created by God.

## The owner's binitarian confession

**GOD IS ONE. THE FATHER AND THE SON ARE ONE — ONE GOD. ONE FAITH. ONE LORD, JESUS CHRIST.**

**THE SON IS ETERNAL AND UNCREATED.** The Son existed with the Father **before the foundation of the world**. The Father's and the Son's creation of the world was their **joint Father–Son work**, before the incarnation.

**THE LORD JESUS IS THE SPIRIT.** OLT is **BINITARIAN, NOT TRINITARIAN**. The statement belongs to the owner's chosen confessional theology; it is not a mechanical rule that every biblical use of `ruach` or `pneuma` directly denotes Jesus.

**The eternal Son became human through Mary.** Human birth does not mean the Son came into existence then. The beginning of the incarnation/human life is distinguished from the eternal existence of the Son.

**The Firstborn Son is not the first created being.** When the owner cites the expression “the beginning of God's creation” in Revelation 3:14 and identifies Christ as the Firstborn Son who became human through Mary, the intended theology does NOT claim that Jesus was the first created thing. Rather, the owner affirms the Son's pre-existence, eternal uncreated identity and active role with the Father in creation.

The wording *“firstborn”* and the Greek `ἀρχή` of Revelation 3:14 must be analysed as **distinct textual and theological questions**. This owner confession does not itself establish a mandatory linguistic translation of `ἀρχή` as *firstborn* or prove a single indisputable scholarly interpretation. It prohibits incorrectly describing OLT's doctrine as *created Son*, *first creature*, or *created at incarnation*.
