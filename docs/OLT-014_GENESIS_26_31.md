# OLT-014 — Genesis 1:26–31 source-first literal research

**Source scope:** 117 original OSHB/WLC Hebrew word tokens, six verses, nine OSHB-source-wayyiqtol annotations and five nonnumeric (preposition + pronoun) tokens, all with original source token IDs and machine-referenced lexicon evidence.

Major lexical boundaries:
- **Gen 1:26:** `na'aseh` is Qal imperfect first common plural with contextual cohortative force, "Let Us make" (asah, NEVER shape). This plural is not, by itself, a conclusive grammatical proof of Trinitarian theology. As Christian intertextual reading, compare John 1:1–3 and Col 1:15–17 without pretending those Greek passages are lexical homonyms of Hebrew asah.
- **Gen 1:27:** original `bara` appears as both wayyiqtol and qatal. OLT uses shaped in each while recording the original difference. The original object forms `et`, `oto`, `otam` respectively give zero gloss, him and them.
- **Gen 1:28:** `vekhivshuha` is Qal imperative 2mp with 3fs object suffix; OLT proposed "and subdue her" refers to the feminine Earth, not "it". The source also contains a nonnumeric lamed + 3mp pronoun `to them`.
- **Gen 1:29:** `nattati` is Qal qatal 1cs ("I gave"), not wayyiqtol; `yihyeh` is imperfect 3ms with an editorial proposed `[he] shall be`, rather than neuter "it". All four preposition + pronominal suffix occurrences across Gen 1:28–30 are recorded in addition to other numeric-lemma words.
- **Gen 1:30:** original sentence is elliptical and remains deliberately awkward in research English. Do not silently invent a finite English verb as if it appeared independently in the Hebrew source.
- **Gen 1:31:** qatal `asah` uses made, never shaped. Hebrew `tov me'od` gives "good exceedingly", and the ordinal in `yom hashishi` includes a definite article.

This completes first proposed word alignment for Genesis 1 after combining OLT-008 through OLT-014, **but not one of these generated research proposals is independently reviewed, owner-finalized or print-published**. The English word choices for other OLT lexical families remain pending. All homonym alternatives must eventually sit vertically BELOW their English lemma, not as inline slash strings.
