# OLT-011 — Genesis 1:11–15 research checkpoint

Source inputs: pinned Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible word IDs/lemmas/morphology and the verified BDB/Strong research cards. All 69 original word tokens are aligned to **research-only** English fragments. 8 source-tagged wayyiqtol forms; 2 grammar-only tokens `בּוֹ` explicitly aligned `in him` without a fictional numeric lemma.

Key grammatical distinctions:
- `תַּדְשֵׁא` (tadshe) is Hiphil jussive, not a narrative past; `דֶּשֶׁא` (deshe) is a related noun. The proposal `sprout sprouts` intentionally preserves a possible root wordplay, **not a proven homonym link**.
- `עֹשֶׂה` (oseh) is Qal participle from **asah**, owner-locked to **making**, never `shaping`. `וַתּוֹצֵא` is wayyiqtol 3fs and is provisionally `she brought forth`.
- `וְהָיוּ` in Genesis 1:14–15 is source-tagged `HC/Vqq3cp` (conjunctive perfect), **not** wayyiqtol. English `[they shall] be` is marked as grammatical/contextual editorial completion.
- In Genesis 1:14–15 `רָקִיעַ` remains **firmament**. `מְאֹרֹת` means lights/luminaries, not an automatic synonym for the light of Gen 1:3.
- `לְמִינוֹ` and `בּוֹ` preserve masculine pronominal suffixes, and supplied English copulas remain bracketed in the full verse proposals.

The machine-verifiable source and project conformance status is not a claim of independent Hebrew linguistic review, owner approval of the new English meanings, authentic verified homonymy, typological interpretation or print-ready ODT/PDF.
