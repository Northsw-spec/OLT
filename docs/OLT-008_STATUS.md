# OLT-008 — First fully token-tracked English research proposal

This checkpoint **does not reuse earlier Genesis 1 prose**. It derives
its seven original-token IDs directly from the pinned OSHB/MT source
ledger and verifies all seven raw lemma and morphological codes.

## Two candidate readings
1. **Plain near-interlinear research:** In the Beginning God — He shaped the Heavens and the Earth.
2. **Optional intertextual draft, NOT source literal:** In the Beginning [of His Power] God — He shaped the Heavens and the Earth.

In Genesis 1:1 `בָּרָא` has source code `HVqp3ms`:
Qal qatal third-person masculine singular, **not wayyiqtol**. It follows the
owner's `bara → shape` lock but is not an example of an OLT wayyiqtol
tense decision. The explicit English `God — He` visibly represents
source grammatical person but reorders the source Hebrew verb-subject
order. Both `אֵת` markers are accounted for with zero English lexical
surface; `וְ` remains `and`.

**The syntax of `בְּרֵאשִׁית` remains debated** between an adverbial and
dependent temporal clause interpretation. The supplied `[of His Power]`
is not a Hebrew lexeme in Gen 1:1; source parallels in Gen 49:3 and Deut
21:17 require a distinct scholarly footnote.

## Seven grammar-only original word tokens
The corpus has 434 original word tokens, but only 427 *numeric lemma
components*. The other seven are **real grammatical words**, with
source-lemmata `b` or `l` and suffix morphology `Sp3ms`, `Sp3mp`
or `Sp2mp`. They are *not* omitted Hebrew lexemes; they are included
as separate original-source review proposals, with actual stable IDs.
They remain **unreviewed** and cannot be called approved translated English.

## Release gate
All Genesis 1:1 English words and grammatical notes are research-only,
not final independent philological certification. All 94 Genesis 1
non-owner-locked English families remain in the research queue, and
full-book publication is blocked.
